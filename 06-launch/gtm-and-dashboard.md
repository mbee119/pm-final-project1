# GTM Launch Plan, RouteLogic (B2B)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | One-Click Compliance Checklist |
| Goal | Engagement |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, Goal: deepen adoption. Drivers already use RouteLogic heavily but skip the multi-screen flow at the moment of delivery and text dispatch instead. Behaviour change is our bottleneck, so we will measure adoption through in-app completion rate (stops completed in the app ÷ all completed stops) and aim for drivers to choose, and keep choosing, the one-click compliance checklist across pilot weeks 2 to 4.
- **Target audience:** Frontline delivery drivers

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, The reach is small and known (drivers and dispatchers at 3 pilot accounts), so targeted comms is enough and keeps the group controlled. The potential revenue impact is significant, but it is not yet proven. As our largest account is trialing a competitor because RouteLogic is too slow at the point of delivery, this pilot is crucial. If drivers adopt the faster flow and daily frontline usage follows,; our premium reporting and compliance features may become  worth paying for. 
A silence would mean drivers will continue with their habit of carrying paper and text and may not notice a quiet change. Adoption stays low and the pilot looks like a failure when the real cause was that nobody knew. Dispatchers who aren’t told of the new feature will keep cross-checking against texts, which removes the efficiency gain.

## Channels
1. **Owned: A 10mins breakfast session before shift start session**
2. **Owned: In-app prompt that walks drivers through the first few confirmations”**
3. **Owned: Dispatcher text nudge**

## Enablement & Assets
1. Short walkthrough (on-site or virtual) to show drivers the one-action flow and address the “just in case the app dies” fear. Assets: Demo script, a 30-60 second demo video or GIF to replay in the call, a one-page quick guide
2. In-app prompt walking drivers through the workflow to guide the driver the first few times they confirm a stop, so the habit shifts at the moment of delivery. Assets: Step-by-step tooltip sequence, “skip” and “don’t show again” states, copy for each step
3. Dispatcher text nudge. Catches drivers at the moment they skip the app. Dispatcher replies to a “stop done” text with a short line pointing to the new checklist. Asset to build: 2 or 3 reply lines, one-page dispatcher briefing

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** 1. In-app completion rate (stops marked complete in the app ÷ all completed stops, in-app + text): Measures whether the app is becoming the place deliveries are recorded.
2. Weekly active adoption: share of treatment drivers who completes at least half their stops through the flow. Measures how many drivers are using the new flow at all
3. Time to complete the compliance step (average minutes per stop, treatment vs control): Measures whether the checklist actually removes the friction
- **Bad signal to watch for:** 1. In-app completion rate flat or falling after the first few weeks. 2. Few treatment drivers using the flow each week,. 3.  time to complete remains flat or increases
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Double down if we hit 20% target or we meet the + 5points (MDE). Iterate if the numbers are not met but direction is positive. Pivot if numbers are not as expected.
